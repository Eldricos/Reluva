# Star Trek: New Worlds: the training missions recorded in a VM - C4 Building, C5 Mining Resources, C6 Power

From the project's own recording of the game played in a virtual machine,
"Tutorials - Others", 0:00-33:06. The game picture is the 640 x 480 game scaled to 712 x 534; the
sound is poor, so some lines are read from the screen. Times are mm:ss in
that video file. Messages and pages are paraphrased. Summary and how this
adds to the earlier review of another player's recording:
[play-vm-tutorials.md](play-vm-tutorials.md) and
[play-tutorials.md](play-tutorials.md).

Font note: in the NEW WORLDS panel the digit 3 renders almost like 5 at this
size. Where arithmetic decides, I give the value the arithmetic supports
(e.g. nitrium 3950, not 5950).

- Construction: after a 4-10 s gap a dark pad appears and spreads; tiny

  round pods round its hexagonal base (`g490.png`, 8:10, five pods on top,
  arms with lights).
