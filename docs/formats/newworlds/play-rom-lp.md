## R8 The Best Laid Plans

Never recorded before.

- **Briefing** (1:45-5:15): stellar cartography (Tabula Rasa), planetary data
  (Hrimfraxi), the objective (obtain a Metar leader), the colony site, a
  warbird breaking up the moon over the Metar colony, the outpost page, an
  APC model turning. The COMMAND panel's objectives: escort the transports
  to the outpost; capture and search the Metar colony core. The stardate
  line names dolamide, but the map's deposits and the Tricorder read
  magnesite; the two exhausted mines (276, 204 and 276, 308) lie on `r8`'s
  kind 5.
- **The start** (5:48): COLONY SIZE **53/50**, over the capacity: the
  abandoned colony the mission places beside the player's (an inert hub,
  storage facility, two accommodation blocks, a shield generator, two power
  generators, two vehicle yards, a clone farm) is the player's and counts.
  Stock 0 / 1000 / 2300 / 0 / 0 / 0 (polycomp and thermoconc only, as the
  data has it), no vehicle yard of its own. Reluva starts so now. By 7:50
  the stock had risen to 0 / 1000 / 2531 / 29 / 250 / 1250 before any
  supply vehicle arrived; the cause wasn't seen.
- **Messages** (the counter reaches 17): the meteor-storm lines (5:40, 6:27,
  8:33, 8:49); supply vehicle 3 arrived first (12:51), then "the Metar are
  on the move" (12:59) and vehicle 3 beamed in (13:01), vehicles 1 and 2 at
  14:58-15:12; "the Metar defences are reacting" at 21:16; mines out at
  32:15 and about 39:50; the counter jumps 15 to 17 at the win (52:30),
  16 most likely the RAS-captured line.
- Whether the player's units move by the script at the start (R8's
  "prepositioners") couldn't be told from the player's own orders.

## The Romulan colony hub's upgrades

Settled by close-ups in J1R, J2R, J3R, J6R, R12 and R14: the **improved
hub** adds a tall ribbed column inside the arch, past its crown (`rch_b`);
the **advanced hub** then adds a dark bladed plate at the arch's foot, its
blades fanning out between the legs with green-lit tips (`rch_c`). From
straight above (R1) the column can't be seen. J6R and J9R start with the
improved hub built. Reluva now shows every race's advanced hub on its hub
([colony.md](colony.md)); upgrades without a model of their own (the
yards', the labs') can be ordered in play now too.
