## What they established

- **The campaigns' order**: the Klingons' K1, K2, J1, J2, J3, K7, J4, K8,
  J6, J7, K12, J8, K14, J9, and the Federation's F1, F2, F3, J1, J2, J3,
  J4, F8, J6, J7, F12, J8, F14, J9 (its videos numbered 1-14, matched by
  their voice clips): level.dat's order, with the joint missions played
  as the side's own and no J5 (the voice folders have none either). The
  loading screen names each "Klingon level NN".
- **Federation briefings open with the transmission page** over the
  emblem, silent, before the first clip (every Federation recording; of
  the Klingon ones only J8 and J3).
- **Briefings** play the mission's voice clips in number order, one per
  step (a lettered clip such as `K12009A` an extra step after its number),
  usually STELLAR CARTOGRAPHY and PLANETARY DATA first. Which page and view
  go with each step is per mission: pages in the table's order, some left
  out, often several clips over the emblem screen with no page. See
  [media.md](media.md).
- **Map positions** in the game (Tricorder readouts, message positions)
  are eighths of a block, row first: the 19 exhausted mines of the recorded
  missions with known maps lie on (one beside) deposits read so. See [gmf.md](gmf.md#map-positions-in-the-game).
- **Mission 14 is played on mission 12's map** (K14's mine on `k12.gmf`).
- **Messages** come in two kinds: generic ones shared by every mission and
  side (a building finished, short of power, short of resources or crew, a
  mine exhausted, all objectives met, in joint missions each rival's hub
  destroyed or mission failed) and the mission script's own (discoveries
  with positions, natives' and off-planet senders' messages, scans and
  research in steps, captures, timed endings).
- **Buildings and units** by name (the selection panel), matched to the
  models: [play-f1.md](play-f1.md#buildings-and-their-models),
  [play-k1.md](play-k1.md#buildings-and-their-models). Upgrades are pieces
  added round a building's first model, each selectable by name.
- **Starting stock** (500, 1000, 2300, 500, 4000, 5000) and colony
  capacity (50, or 90 in later missions).
